Help

Frequently asked questions

View all FAQs
Where is IXORA Homestay located?

At Niduvaloor Gate, Niduvaloor, Kannur district, Kerala — a private 2 BHK homestay and event venue surrounded by nature.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early or late timing can be requested subject to availability.

How do I book and what does it cost?

Send your dates on Book Now or WhatsApp +91 80757 71824. Day stay, overnight and event packages are quoted on enquiry based on date and guest count.

Can we celebrate birthdays or engagements here?

Yes. Host birthdays, engagements, family gatherings and get-togethers. Stage, photo point, campfire and BBQ grill can be added to your celebration.

Is kitchen and parking included?

Yes. Guests have access to the private kitchen, dining space and on-site parking with the booking.

What places can we explore nearby?

Hills, waterfalls, temples, St. Angelo Fort and Muzhappilangad Drive-in Beach are all within reach. See the full list on our Explore page.
