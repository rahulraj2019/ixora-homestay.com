Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before booking a stay or celebration at IXORA, Niduvaloor Gate.

Where is IXORA Homestay located?

IXORA is at Niduvaloor Gate, Niduvaloor, in Kannur district, Kerala. It is a private 2 BHK homestay and event venue surrounded by nature.

What is included in the stay?

You get the full private house: 2 bedrooms, living area, kitchen, dining room, private courtyard, games, photo point and parking. Stage, campfire and BBQ grill can be arranged for celebrations.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early arrival or late departure can be requested if the schedule allows.

How do I book IXORA?

Send your dates, guest count and booking type via the Book Now form or WhatsApp +91 80757 71824. You can also call +91 94968 50582. We confirm availability and rates directly.

How much does a stay or event cost?

Day stay, overnight stay and event packages are quoted on enquiry. Rates depend on your dates, guest count and any event add-ons. Weekday and weekend pricing may differ.

Can I host a birthday, engagement or family gathering?

Yes. IXORA hosts birthdays, engagements, family functions, friends get-togethers and custom events. Ask for stage space, photo point, grill and campfire when you plan your celebration.

Is the kitchen available for guests?

Yes. The private kitchen is included with your booking so you can prepare meals, and the dining room is ready for group meals.

Is parking available?

Yes. On-site parking is available for guests arriving by car.

Are campfire and BBQ included?

Campfire, BBQ grill, stage, decoration, games and the photo point can be added to any stay or celebration. Mention what you need when you enquire.

What nearby places can we visit?

Popular trips from here include Palakkayam Thattu, Paithalmala, Ezharakund and Alakapuri waterfalls, Muthappan Temple, Vismaya, Rajarajeswara Temple, Madayi Para, St. Angelo Fort and Muzhappilangad Drive-in Beach. See the full list on our Explore page.

Is there an extra guest charge?

Extra guest charges may apply beyond the base occupancy confirmed for your booking. Share your final headcount when you enquire so we can quote correctly.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms are confirmed with your booking and may vary by date and package. Ask us on WhatsApp or phone for the policy that applies to your reservation.
